How these numbers are calculated
Funded
Sum of funded amounts on deals with a funded date in the last 90 days, counted once per deal.
Open pipeline
Requested amount (or selected amount, once chosen) of every application not yet Funded or Declined.
Ready to submit
Open files whose document package is complete and verified, with at least one matching program and no open submission.
Awaiting providers
Submissions sent to a provider with no decision recorded yet (Submitted or Received).
Provider approval rate
Approved, countered or funded submissions ÷ all submissions with a recorded decision, for decisions in the last 90 days.
Days to fund
Median days from application opened to funded, for deals funded in the last 90 days.
Capital deployed
POP Direct only: principal still outstanding in live POP-funded deals = remaining balance × (amount ÷ payback). Defaulted deals stay deployed until written off.
Capital available
Committed capital per source minus capital deployed from that source, summed.
Collection rate
POP Direct only: scheduled payments collected ÷ scheduled payments due in the last 90 days (retries count as collections, not as extra payments due).
Late or worse
POP Direct only: remaining balance on deals whose servicing status is Late, Delinquent or Default.
